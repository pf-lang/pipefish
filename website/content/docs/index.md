Welcome to Pipefish!

The documents in this section fully document the language and the Pipefish standard library. They are arranged thematically rather than in the order suitable for a tutorial, and to learn the language you may wish to samle a little of the various sections in turn, rather than reading it in order and trying e.g. to assimilate the entire type system before moving on.

Some additional documents <a href="/articles/">Articles</a> will give you some additional insight into why Pipefish exists and why it's the way it is, but it's not necessary to read them to learn the language.
